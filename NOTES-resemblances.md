# Resemblances (notes only — nothing here is a source for any Lean statement)

Jeromie Beasley's own papers (geometric flux and magnetic charge, IQGT, transport, colour) were
used only as a lens for noticing things in Maxwell. Nothing from them is imported, restated, or
proved in the `Maxwell` library, and no resemblance below is evidence of priority or support.

* Art. 643's force law keeps a magnetic-matter term `αm` derived from the stress before Art. 644
  removes it by the Ampère–Weber hypothesis. This resembles an interest in magnetic charge; in
  Maxwell it is Poisson's "imaginary magnetic matter", his empirical position is that the total
  magnetism of a magnet is zero (Art. 377) and that no pole can be cut out (Art. 832), and Art. 644
  removes the term. Resemblance only.
